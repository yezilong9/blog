Cloudflare Pages: Deploying a Free-Forever Node, Part Two

Utils 2026-06-03

This tutorial is for learning about Cloudflare deployment, not for commercial use.

Register with Cloudflare

Cloudflare is a well-known CDN provider with services such as reverse proxies, tunneling, and SSL. Its individual free plan covers the learning setup in this tutorial.

Workers and Pages are among its services. Here, we use Pages to deploy a VLESS node service.

Visit Cloudflare and choose the free plan near the bottom of the page.

The registration process is straightforward. Remember to verify your email.

Create a KV namespace

  1. In your Cloudflare account, select Storage & databases → Workers KV, enter a namespace name, and click Create.

Create a Pages application

  1. Download the latest project ZIP: edgetunnel-main.zip.
  2. Select Compute & AI → Workers & Pages → Create application.

  1. Click Get started.

  1. Enter a project name and click Create project.

  1. Upload the ZIP you downloaded, then click Deploy site.

  1. After deployment succeeds, click Continue to project.

  1. Open Settings → Variables and Secrets to add a variable.

  1. Add ADMIN and set its value to the administrator login password. Click Save.

  1. Bind the KV namespace created earlier. The binding's variable name must be uppercase KV.

  1. After saving, click Create deployment.

  1. Upload the project again, then click Save and Deploy.

  1. The success page confirms deployment and provides the production URL.

  1. Open the deployed URL in a browser and append /login, for example https://edt-8pd.pages.dev/login. Sign in with the password you set in the variable.

Congratulations, your own deployment is ready. Subscription setup is outside the scope of this walkthrough.

Finally, do not share your account or connection links with others, or use them for profit. Doing so may violate criminal law and have serious consequences.
